Guide · Learn the method

The 50/30/20 rule, in plain English.

Split after-tax income three ways: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. It is not a diet — it is a map. Here is exactly how one real family uses it.

50% — Needs

Rent, groceries, transport, utilities, insurance, minimum bills. If needs pass 60%, the fix is usually housing or transport, not willpower.

$2,600

30% — Wants

Dining out, streaming, trips, hobbies, new clothes. Wants are not waste — a budget with zero fun is a budget nobody keeps.

$1,560

20% — Savings

Emergency fund first, then retirement and big goals. Automate it on payday so saving happens before spending can.
